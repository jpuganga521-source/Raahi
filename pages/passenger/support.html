QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently asked

How do I report a fare issue?+

Choose “Payment or fare question” in the form and include the ride reference. Never share your full payment credentials.

I left something behind+

Submit a “Lost item” request and include your ride ID and a description of the item. Support can guide you through next steps.

Can I contact my rider?+

For an active trip, open My rides and use the rider contact options. These controls are demo-only for now.

Who reviews my complaint?+

Raahi's intended process is to review passenger and rider concerns fairly. The admin review flow will be part of a future phase.
